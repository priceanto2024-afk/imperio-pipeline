Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

IMPERIO Pipeline ("the tool") is a private application used by a single owner to manage their own YouTube channels.

What data the tool accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the tool uses these YouTube API scopes: youtube.upload to upload the owner's videos, youtube.force-ssl to set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and schedules on the owner's videos, and yt-analytics.readonly to read performance reports of the owner's channels.

How the data is used and stored

OAuth tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are used solely to publish the owner's content and to review its performance. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Users

The tool has no public users. It does not collect data from viewers or from any person other than the channel owner.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
